Cancellation and refunds

Plans never renew on their own, so there is nothing to cancel. Here is exactly when you get money back and how fast.

01At a glance

Always refunded

Paid, but minutes never arrived. Charged twice for one checkout.

Refunded on request

Within 7 days of paying, if under 10 of that plan's minutes were used.

Not refunded

Minutes already used, or unused days later in a period.

02Cancellation

Paid plans are one-time prepaid payments for 30 days, not subscriptions. Nothing renews or is debited automatically, so there is nothing to cancel. If you don't pay again, your plan ends after 30 days and you return to any free minutes left.

03When we refund

  • Activation failure: your payment succeeded but your build minutes were not added, and we can't fix it within 2 working days. Full refund.
  • Duplicate payment: you were charged more than once for the same checkout. The extra payment is refunded in full.
  • Change of mind: you ask within 7 days of payment and have used less than 10 build minutes from that plan. Full refund. %%REFUND_LIMITS_NOTE%%
  • A fault on our side: if a build stopped because of our systems (not your AI provider or repository), we restore the minutes it used, on request.

04When we don't

We don't refund build minutes already used, builds that failed because of your AI account, API key, repository or designs, unused minutes or days after the 7-day window, or your AI provider's charges, which it bills directly.

05How to ask

Email %%SUPPORT_EMAIL%% from your account email with your Razorpay payment reference (it starts with pay_) and the reason. We reply within 2 working days.

06Timelines

Approved refunds are started within 3 working days and returned to the original payment method through Razorpay. Banks and card issuers usually credit them within 5–7 working days. UPI refunds are often faster.

07What a refund changes

A full refund ends the 30-day period that payment bought, together with any minutes carried into it, and your account returns to any free minutes left. Refunding an earlier payment does not affect a later period you paid for.
