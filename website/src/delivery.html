01Nothing ships physically

Design Engineer is a digital service. Everything you buy is delivered online, to your account and your GitHub repository.

02What is delivered

A paid plan adds build minutes to your account for 30 days. The code from each build arrives in the GitHub repository and branch you choose, as a pull request.

03When it is delivered

  • Build minutes: usually within a minute of Razorpay confirming your payment. They appear in the plugin under Plans & minutes, and the checkout page confirms it.
  • Code: when a build finishes, which is within 15 minutes of it starting.

04Where it is available

Anywhere you can use the Figma desktop app and GitHub. Checkout currently accepts payments in Indian rupees.

05Delivery charges

None. The plan price is the full price.

06If something doesn't arrive

If your minutes haven't appeared 30 minutes after a successful payment, email %%SUPPORT_EMAIL%% with your payment reference. If we can't add them within 2 working days, you get a full refund under our Cancellation and refund policy.
