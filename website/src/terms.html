01Who we are

These terms govern your use of the Design Engineer Figma plugin, website and related services ("Design Engineer", "we", "us"). Design Engineer is operated by Hari Prasad, an individual based in Bangalore, India. By installing the plugin, creating an account or making a payment, you agree to these terms.

02The service

Design Engineer reads the Figma frames you select, sends them to a coding agent on our build machines, and delivers the resulting React JS or React Native code to a branch of a GitHub repository you choose, as a pull request. Generated code can contain errors. Review it before you use or merge it.

03Your account

Give accurate details and keep your login secure. One free trial is allowed per person, and creating extra accounts to get more free minutes is not permitted. You must be at least 18, or have a parent's or guardian's consent, to buy a plan.

04Accounts you connect

Builds use the AI account you connect (for example a ChatGPT account through Codex, or an Anthropic, Google or other provider API key) and your GitHub account. Their terms govern your use of them, and your AI provider bills its own charges. We store connected credentials encrypted, only to run your builds, and you can disconnect them at any time.

05Plans, build minutes and payment

  • The Free plan includes 40 build minutes, once per account. They do not reset.
  • Plus (₹799, 100 build minutes) and Studio (₹1,699, 200 build minutes) are prepaid plans that last 30 days from payment. Prices are in Indian rupees and include applicable taxes %%GST_NOTE%%.
  • Build minutes are measured per second, from when your build machine starts until the build finishes or stops. Queue time is not counted. One build can run for up to 15 minutes, and unused reserved time returns to your balance.
  • A build that fails or is stopped is charged for the time it actually ran.
  • Plans do not renew automatically. Buying the same plan before your period ends adds 30 days to your current end date and carries unused minutes over. Switching plans before your period ends starts a new 30-day period with unused minutes carried over. Minutes left when a period ends without renewal expire.
  • Build minutes have no cash value and cannot be transferred, sold or exchanged.
  • Payments are processed by Razorpay. We never see or store your card, UPI or bank details.
