01Who is responsible

This policy explains what personal data Design Engineer collects, why, and your choices. Design Engineer is operated by Hari Prasad, Bangalore, India, who is responsible for your data (the data fiduciary under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023).

02What we collect

  • Account details: your email address, and your name if you sign in with Google.
  • Connected accounts: access tokens for GitHub and for the AI account you connect, stored encrypted.
  • Design data: the structure, styles, text and screenshots of the Figma frames you choose to send.
  • Project and build records: repository and branch names, build status, build minutes used and errors.
  • Payment records: plan, amount, status and Razorpay references. Razorpay collects card, UPI and bank details, not us.
  • Technical data: basic request logs (such as IP address and time) kept for security and troubleshooting.

03Why we use it

To run your builds and deliver code, measure build minutes and apply your plan, process payments and refunds, prevent fraud and abuse, answer support requests and meet legal and tax obligations. We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or use your designs or code to train AI models.

04Who we share it with

Only the providers needed to run Design Engineer, under their own privacy terms:

  • Supabase for sign-in and the database
  • Cloudflare for hosting and build machines
  • Razorpay for payments
  • GitHub to deliver code to your repository
  • The AI provider you connect, to generate code
  • Google, only if you choose Google sign-in

Some providers may process data outside India %%DATA_REGION_NOTE%%. We may also disclose data when the law requires it.

05How long we keep it

Account, project and build records are kept while your account is open and deleted within 30 days of a deletion request. Connected-account tokens are deleted as soon as you disconnect them. Payment records are kept as long as tax and accounting laws require, usually up to 8 years.

06Security

Connected credentials are encrypted before storage, each user can reach only their own records, and billing changes are made only by our server after verifying with Razorpay. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your data, we notify you and the authorities as the law requires.

07Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your personal data, withdraw consent, or nominate someone to act for you, by writing to %%SUPPORT_EMAIL%%. You can also disconnect GitHub or your AI account in the plugin at any time.

08Cookies

This website uses only the cookies it needs to work. The plugin keeps your session in Figma's storage on your device.
